# Contract Playbook (Nimbus Cloud - buyer side)

## Limitation of Liability
- Standard: vendor liability cap of at least 12 months of fees paid.
- Fallback: cap of 6 months of fees, but with uncapped liability for data breaches and confidentiality.
- Red line: any cap below 3 months of fees, or excluding data breaches from the cap carve-outs.

## Indemnification
- Standard: vendor indemnifies us for third-party IP infringement claims and data breaches caused by the vendor.
- Red line: mutual indemnities that make us indemnify the vendor for their own negligence.

## Term and Termination
- Standard: 12-month initial term; we may terminate for convenience with 30 days' notice.
- Fallback: termination for convenience with 90 days' notice.
- Red line: auto-renewal longer than 12 months, or no termination right for material breach.

## Payment Terms
- Standard: net 45 days from invoice.
- Fallback: net 30.
- Red line: payment in advance for more than one year, or late fees above 1% per month.

## Data Protection
- Standard: vendor processes personal data only on our instructions, signs a DPA, notifies breaches within 48 hours.
- Red line: breach notification later than 72 hours, or the right to use our data for training or analytics.

## Governing Law
- Standard: laws of Delaware, USA. Fallback: New York or England and Wales.
- Red line: jurisdictions where we have no legal presence.
