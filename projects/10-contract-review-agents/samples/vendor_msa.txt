MASTER SERVICES AGREEMENT between DataPipe Analytics Ltd ("Vendor") and Nimbus Cloud Inc ("Customer").

1. TERM. This Agreement starts on the Effective Date and continues for 36 months, renewing automatically for
successive 36-month periods unless either party gives 180 days' written notice of non-renewal.

2. FEES AND PAYMENT. Customer shall pay all fees for the full initial term annually in advance. Invoices are due
within 15 days. Late payments accrue interest at 2% per month.

3. DATA. Vendor will process Customer Data to provide the services and may use aggregated and de-identified
Customer Data to improve and train its products. Vendor will notify Customer of a security breach within
5 business days of confirming it.

4. LIMITATION OF LIABILITY. Vendor's total liability under this Agreement shall not exceed the fees paid in the
one (1) month preceding the claim. This cap applies to all claims, including breaches of data protection obligations.

5. INDEMNIFICATION. Vendor shall indemnify Customer against third-party claims alleging that the services
infringe intellectual property rights. Customer shall indemnify Vendor against all claims arising from use of
the services, including claims caused by Vendor's negligence.

6. TERMINATION. Either party may terminate for material breach not cured within 60 days of notice.

7. GOVERNING LAW. This Agreement is governed by the laws of England and Wales.
