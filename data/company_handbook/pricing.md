# Nimbus Cloud Pricing

Nimbus Cloud is a fictional managed hosting company used throughout these tutorials.

## Plans

### Starter
- Price: $19 per month, billed monthly.
- Includes 2 vCPU, 4 GB RAM, 50 GB SSD storage.
- Up to 3 projects and 1 team member.
- Community support only (forum).

### Pro
- Price: $79 per month, or $790 per year (two months free).
- Includes 8 vCPU, 16 GB RAM, 250 GB SSD storage.
- Unlimited projects and up to 10 team members.
- Email support with a 24-hour first-response target.

### Enterprise
- Custom pricing, starting at $1,500 per month.
- Dedicated hardware, SSO (SAML), audit logs, and a 99.99% uptime SLA.
- 24/7 phone support with a 1-hour first-response target for Severity 1 issues.

## Add-ons
- Extra storage: $0.10 per GB per month.
- Bandwidth above the included 1 TB: $0.05 per GB.
- Managed PostgreSQL: from $25 per month.

## Discounts
- Students and non-profits receive 50% off the Pro plan after verification.
- Annual billing saves roughly 17% compared with monthly billing.
