# Refund and Cancellation Policy

## Cancelling a subscription
You can cancel at any time from **Settings > Billing > Cancel plan**. Your plan stays active until the
end of the current billing period. Data is retained for 30 days after cancellation, then permanently deleted.

## Refunds
- Monthly plans: refunds are available within **14 days** of the first payment only.
- Annual plans: a **prorated refund** is available within the first 60 days.
- Add-on usage (storage, bandwidth) is never refunded.
- Enterprise contracts follow the terms of the signed order form.

## How to request a refund
Email billing@nimbus.example with your account ID and invoice number. Refunds are processed within
5-7 business days to the original payment method.

## Chargebacks
Opening a chargeback suspends the account immediately until the dispute is resolved.
