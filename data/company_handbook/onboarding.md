# Employee Onboarding Guide

## First week
1. Day 1: laptop setup with IT, accounts for Slack, GitHub and the HR portal.
2. Day 2: security training (mandatory, must be completed within 3 days of starting).
3. Day 3-5: shadow your onboarding buddy and ship a first small change.

## Time off
- Full-time employees get 25 days of paid vacation per year plus public holidays.
- Vacation accrues monthly; up to 5 unused days roll over to the next year.
- Sick leave is unlimited but more than 3 consecutive days requires a doctor's note.

## Remote work
Nimbus is remote-first. Employees receive a one-time $1,000 home-office stipend and a $50 monthly
internet allowance. Core collaboration hours are 14:00-17:00 UTC.

## Expenses
Submit expenses in the HR portal within 30 days. Purchases above $500 need manager approval first.
