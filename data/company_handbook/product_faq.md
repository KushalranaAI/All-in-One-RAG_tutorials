# Product FAQ

## Which regions do you support?
We run in five regions: us-east, us-west, eu-central (Frankfurt), ap-south (Mumbai) and ap-southeast (Singapore).

## Can I use my own domain?
Yes. Custom domains are free on all plans, with automatic HTTPS certificates from Let's Encrypt.

## Which languages are supported?
Any language that runs in a container. We provide buildpacks for Python, Node.js, Go, Java, Ruby and PHP.

## Do you offer a free trial?
Yes, a 14-day free trial of the Pro plan with no credit card required.

## How do I contact support?
Starter users use the community forum. Pro users email support@nimbus.example. Enterprise customers
also get a dedicated Slack channel and 24/7 phone support.

## What is the maximum upload size?
Single deploy artifacts are limited to 2 GB.
